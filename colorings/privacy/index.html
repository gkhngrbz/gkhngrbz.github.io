The short version for parents

  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library access.
  • This version sends no analytics or usage statistics to us.

Colorings is designed for preschool and early-elementary children and the grown-ups who look after them. We follow the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple’s rules for apps made for kids.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app never asks for, a child’s or a parent’s name, email address, phone number, postal address, birthday, photos, voice, contacts, precise location or any free text. The app has no accounts, no chat, no social features and no way for a child to share information with other people.

The app asks for no device permissions: no camera, microphone, photo library, location, contacts, notifications or tracking.

3. Information stored only on your device

To work, the app keeps a few files in its private storage on the device. They are never sent to us.

WhatWhy
Artwork (the colors and strokes of each picture, and small preview images)So your child can keep and continue their pictures in “My Art”.

You can delete this information at any time: in the app, open the Parent area and choose Delete all artwork or Reset stars and unlocks, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes all of its files.

4. Ads

Colorings is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). There are two kinds:

  • Optional reward videos that a child can choose to watch to unlock a picture or the glitter crayon. Nothing is unlocked by a purchase.
  • Occasional full-screen ads at natural pauses, such as after finishing a picture or when leaving one. They are limited per day, spaced several minutes apart, and never appear in the middle of coloring.

Every ad request is configured for children:

  • it is tagged for child-directed treatment, which tells Google to handle it in a way that is consistent with COPPA;
  • it asks for non-personalized ads only: no interest-based advertising, no remarketing and no advertising profiles;
  • the maximum ad content rating is G (suitable for general audiences);
  • the app never shows Apple’s tracking prompt, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the app or to Google;
  • ads start with the sound muted.

What Google may receive when an ad is requested

To deliver an ad, Google’s software in the app automatically sends some technical information to Google. Based on Google’s published disclosures, this can include:

  • the device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate a general (not precise) location;
  • an identifier that is limited to this app’s developer on this device (Apple’s “identifier for vendor”), not the advertising identifier;
  • information about the ads shown and how they were used, for example whether a video was watched to the end;
  • diagnostic and performance information, such as crash logs and load times.

Under COPPA, this information is used only for “support for internal operations”: serving contextual (not behavioral) ads, limiting how often ads are shown, confirming that a reward video was completed, measuring ad delivery, preventing fraud, keeping the service secure and fixing problems. It is not used to build a profile of your child or to target ads based on their behavior. We never receive this information ourselves.

Google’s handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you would rather not have any ad requests made, keep the device offline while your child plays. Colorings works fully offline: all pictures, colors and saved artwork are available without an internet connection. Only reward videos need to be online.

Ads may contain links. Tapping an ad can open the App Store or a website outside the app, which is run by the advertiser under its own privacy policy.

5. Analytics

This version of the app sends no analytics, usage statistics or crash reports to us. If a future version adds them, they will contain no names, no device identifiers and no advertising identifiers, and we will update this policy and the in-app privacy summary before that version is released.

6. Features that only a grown-up can use

The Parent area, sharing a picture and rating the app are behind a grown-up check (a multiplication question). Behind that check:

  • Sharing opens your device’s share sheet. The picture goes only where you choose, for example Messages or Photos, and the app you pick handles it under its own policy. We never receive it.
  • Rating the app uses Apple’s standard rating window. Apple handles any rating or review.
  • Links to this policy, the terms of use or support email open outside the app.

7. Data retention

This is our written data retention policy:

  • On the device. Artwork, progress and settings stay on the device until a grown-up deletes them in the Parent area or deletes the app. We do not keep copies.
  • With us. We hold no personal information from the app. If you email us, we keep your message and email address only as long as needed to answer you, and delete them no later than 12 months after the conversation ends, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • With Google. Information sent with ad requests is kept by Google under its own retention policies, described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Your rights as a parent

Because we do not collect personal information from your child, we have none to review or delete on our side. Everything the app stores is on your device, and you can delete it at any time as described in section 3. If you have any questions, or believe we may have received information about your child, contact us and we will look into it and delete anything we find.

We do not ask a child to provide more information than is needed to play, and no feature depends on giving personal information.

9. Users outside the United States

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Turkey or another region with data protection laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. We do not hold personal data from the app, so most requests will concern the information Google receives with ad requests. Google processes it as described in its privacy policy, and you can exercise your rights with Google directly. You can also contact us, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

App files are kept in the app’s private storage on the device, protected by the operating system. Ad requests are handled by Google’s software over encrypted connections.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. If a change would mean collecting personal information from children, we would first ask for a parent’s verifiable consent as required by law.
